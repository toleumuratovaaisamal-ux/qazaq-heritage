Cultural Landmarks

Kazakh Yurt (Киіз үй)

A portable, felt-covered wood lattice dwelling traditionally used by nomads in the steppes of Central Asia.

Tamgaly Petroglyphs

A UNESCO World Heritage site featuring thousands of ancient rock carvings dating back to the Bronze Age.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Qazaq Heritage?
Qazaq Heritage is an educational web platform created to celebrate and share Kazakhstan's history and cultural monuments.
Where are these sites located?
The heritage sites are spread across various regions of Kazakhstan, including Turkistan, Almaty region, and Burabay.
How can I visit these monuments?
You can find official travel routes and guided tours provided by local tourism organizations in Kazakhstan.
